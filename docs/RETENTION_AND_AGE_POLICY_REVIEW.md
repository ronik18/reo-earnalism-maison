## All-ages reading is different from child-account data collection

Do not replace “any age” with an unapproved18+ platform restriction. Equally, an unrestricted audience preference does not remove consent/notice obligations when processing children's data. A child can read with a guardian without automatically requiring a separate child profile, date of birth, tracking identifier or marketing consent. An adult-labelled account alone is not an exemption if the service knowingly collects a child's personal information through it. Notes/progress/support messages and essential network identifiers still require appropriate assessment; an anonymous page is not necessarily zero data processing.

### Verified primary-source position as of18 November2026

- **India:** the India Code Act publication includes commencement notes: sections7–17, including section9, commence18months after13November2025, i.e. **13May2027**. Section9 is therefore not being presented as already commenced today. The enacted Act defines a child as under18 and section9 provides parental/guardian verification and restrictions, subject to its terms/exemptions when applicable. Existing other applicable duties are not dismissed during the staged period. [India Code Act and commencement notes](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/22037/1/a2023-22.pdf), [official commencement notification](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_45_0_00003_2023-22_1763464807080&filename=c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf&type=hindinotification).
- **United States:** COPPA applies to covered child-directed services under13 and services with actual knowledge they collect such children's personal information. FTC guidance generally requires notice and verifiable parental consent **before** collection, subject to limited exceptions; parental review/deletion, minimization and retention protections also matter. A checkbox saying “parent agrees” is not automatically verified permission. Current amended-rule guidance must be used, not an old claim that unrestricted signup is safe. [FTC COPPA FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions), [FTC rule](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/rules/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-coppa).
- **EU/EEA:** GDPR Article8 concerns consent-based processing for an information-society service offered directly to a child: the default threshold is16, and member states may lower it but not below13. Below the applicable threshold, parental authorization and reasonable verification are required for that consent basis. This is not a universal prohibition on reading, nor a blanket legal basis for all child data. Check applicable member-state threshold and other bases/requirements; do not select13 globally. [Official GDPR Article8](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/).

These three primary-source checks are not a completed47-jurisdiction children-law matrix. UK and other active jurisdictions require their own applicable assessment before child-account availability claims. Removing AE/CN/pending regions does not itself resolve this issue.
