## Material terms actually reviewed

The [developer terms hub](https://www.kidswebservices.com/en-US/developer-terms-hub) was retrieved with BrowserAct after direct fetch returned403. Reviewed primary documents: [General Terms](https://www.kidswebservices.com/general-terms), last updated13May2026; [Parent Verification Terms](https://www.kidswebservices.com/parent-verification-terms), effective28August2025; [DPA](https://www.kidswebservices.com/data-processing-addendum); [Consent Management Terms](https://www.kidswebservices.com/consent-management-terms). Current supplied content, not a click-through acceptance receipt, is the evidence.

Parent Verification terms2/5 distinguish initial email/API operations as processor activity from later adult verification/AgeGraph activity as independent-controller processing. Clause3 does not guarantee parental relationship, exact age or national legality of a verification method. Adult verification alone therefore cannot be labeled universal guardian authority. Methods may change on notice. General terms impose business warranties, liability allocation and service-related content licensing; consent and verification involve distinct data/roles. No book content or draft would be supplied by the proposed integration.

The DPA distinguishes controller-to-controller and controller-to-processor EU/UK transfer clauses. Those instruments do not automatically satisfy other national routes or domestic-storage rules. A hashed reusable AgeGraph record remains a data flow; it is not anonymous simply because the email is hashed. [Method documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/kids-web-services/parent-verification-service/pv-service-verification-methods) and [official support](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-41468510/c-38656246/a11433351) explain geographic options and possible small parent-side card charges/authorizations. Do not promise no financial effect or demand card/identity documents for every reader.
