## Resolved: an external verification provider is not a universal prerequisite

The smallest route is to select a legally supported **method per purpose and country**, then reuse the existing authority/claim/withdrawal machinery. An Epic organization only opens a particular vendor’s support/onboarding; it supplies no legal permission. KWS is optional. Do not introduce a generic requirement for cryptographic vendor assertions or separate custody documents where the selected law already recognizes a proportionate direct method. Conversely, mailbox control alone is not every required method.

### US: direct methods exist, including a lower-data conditional email route

Current [16 CFR 312.5](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.5), displayed current to 19 November 2026, expressly permits signed parent forms returned by scan/mail/fax, trained-staff video or toll-free calls, and other listed methods. It also permits email-plus or text-plus where the operator does not “disclose” covered child information. Initial parent/child contact collection solely to obtain permission has a bounded exception with deletion if permission is not obtained within a reasonable time. This supplies a no-new-verifier implementation candidate, not an already completed consent flow.

The [312.2 definitions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.2) exclude qualifying internal-operations support recipients from “disclosure,” subject to restrictions on other uses. Therefore do not decide email-plus eligibility merely by counting cloud providers. Classify actual contracts, data, purposes and onward uses; optional Google identity and any independent-controller processing need separate treatment. The nonprofit exclusion depends on Section 5 FTC status, not reader price. Neutral child identification is separately relevant to the amended mixed-audience definition; labeling all users adults is not a lawful shortcut.

The [FTC FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions), B.5 and I.6, confirms nonprofit limits and says proper listed card/ID methods need not add separate proof that the adult is the actual parent. Avoid converting a useful fraud/household-binding control into an unsupported universal legal requirement for birth/custody records. The FAQ warns that it has not fully incorporated the 2025 amendment; use the current rule for the operative methods. Free Indian proprietorship operation has not been shown to qualify for nonprofit exemption. US state-law coverage remains unresolved.

**Smallest next work:** a parent-initiated, purpose/child-slot-bound direct notice and signed-return form is a supported US candidate without vendor onboarding; assess minimum form/evidence retention and actual precollection timing. Email-plus could be smaller only after a documented no-disclosure determination. No automatic charge, facial scan or government ID is necessary merely to explore these methods. Do not activate a method globally from the US rule.

### Canada: assess actual purposes; a literary exception is narrow

[PIPEDA](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/FullText.html), current to29October2026, section4 distinguishes commercial activity and processing solely for journalistic/artistic/literary purposes. This is a real scope question, not a blanket literary-library exemption: account administration, identity, security, progress and learning records need purpose-level analysis. A zero price or a book catalog does not establish that all associated processing is solely literary. No universal Canadian in-country verification vendor or representative requirement was established.
