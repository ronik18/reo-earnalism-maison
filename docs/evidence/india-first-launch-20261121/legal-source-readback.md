## Narrow wording and claim limits

- The adult native/password and independent-account checkbox currently says that the person agrees to terms and **understands** the notice and necessary overseas processing. The linked privacy notice says the adult agrees to necessary processing. Parent and final-child checkboxes already explicitly authorize it. To remove unnecessary ambiguity at negligible UI cost, use “I agree to the service terms and the necessary account and overseas processing described in the privacy notice” for adult/password/independent/signup equivalents, while retaining the separate password-purpose choice. This is wording precision, not a demand for an additional provider, separate paid consent tool or repeated checkbox for every recipient.
- Existing Google sign-in can fetch an unknown caller's basic identity before rejecting new-account creation. Call this an existing-account sign-in lookup, **not** a claim that every new/child network byte is rejected before OAuth. The signup path itself requires the adult choice before redirect. No reviewed current SPDI rule establishes a universal zero-byte pre-parent intake safe harbor.
- The region middleware rejects before downstream application body/session handling; Cloudflare/TLS/network metadata and security-event handling still occur. Connection country is not citizenship, domicile or parent capacity. The source and service-policy contract correctly say this; preserve that distinction in the receipt.
- A permission hash, email check or local synthetic acceptance must not be described as government identity verification, established legal custody or compliance with future DPDP Rule10. This method records the adult's declarations and specific electronic permission under the selected present-law flow.
