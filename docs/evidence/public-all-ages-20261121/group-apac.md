# APAC public/all-ages qualification research — draft, 21 November 2026

Research group: IN, BD, BN, BT, HK, ID, JP, KH, KR, LA, LK, MM, MO, MV, MY, NP, NZ, PH, PK, SG, TH, TW. Official sources were searched/opened in this run; source-access limits are identified below. This is a bounded applicability and implementation assessment, not a local legal opinion or a regional PASS. Where an authority hosts an English translation labelled unofficial, the original enactment controls.

The scope now includes a public all-ages free library account service, private reading progress/notes and international Render/Neon/Cloudflare/Google processing. Earlier India-only adult-staff facts cannot qualify that expanded audience. Free access does not remove statutory territorial, child, sensitive-data or transfer requirements. Reading notes can reveal religion, health, politics or other protected information; account identifiers/security logs remain personal data. A privacy-notice version and password-purpose checkbox do not establish consent for every purpose or lawful consent by a child.

No new age restriction, guardian flow, language change, territorial reduction, filing, appointment, purchase or provider mutation is authorized by this draft. Preserve all47 availability/all-ages intent, draft book and legacy platform. Physical device UAT remains deferred/nonblocking. Any required child/guardian behavior must be put to the owner as a concrete minimal proposal, rather than silently restricting the audience.

## Commencement corrections that materially change the old matrix

- **Bangladesh:** current instrument is **Personal Data Protection Act 2026, Act63 of2026**, not the old2025 draft/Ordinance alone. Section1(3) deems most provisions effective6November2025; sections23 and31–35 await a notified date after18months from issuance. Section1(2)(c) reaches overseas processing associated with offering services to people located in Bangladesh. Section2(19) defines child as under18 or a government-set age. [Official consolidated preliminary chapter](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1692/act-chapter-print-2693.html), [government gazette record](https://dpp.gov.bd/bgpress/index.php/document/get_extraordinary/61156).
- **Brunei:** the Order is enacted and its substantive Parts3–9, section42 and Schedules1–5 commenced **1January2026** under S11/2025. Do not leave substantive compliance marked a future2025 draft. [Official commencement notification](https://www.agc.gov.bn/AGC%20Images/LAWS/Gazette_PDF/2025/EN/S%2011_2025%5BE%5D.pdf).
- **Sri Lanka:** the2025 amendment replaced the old commencement mechanism. The DPA's current status, dated16November2026, identifies Gazette2498/16 of22July2026: sections2/3 and PartsI/III commence **1January2027**. PartsII/IV/VII have no appointed date in the Orders reviewed by the DPA. PartsV/VI/VIII/IX/X already operate. The old18March2025 announcement is superseded, and1January2027 is not whole-Act commencement. [DPA current background/status](https://www.dpa.gov.lk/Background), [amending Act22/2025](https://www.dpa.gov.lk/acts/22-2025_E_251104_201549.pdf).
- **Taiwan:** the authority's present consolidated-law status explicitly says the11November2025 amendments have not yet commenced; Executive Yuan appointment remains required. Apply currently effective provisions, including the existing security framework, rather than treating the displayed repeal of Article27 as operative. [Authority status/history](https://law.pdpc.gov.tw/LawContentSource.aspx?id=FL010627), [current authority commentary](https://www.pdpc.gov.tw/News_Html/100/).
- **India:** GSR843(E) phases core DPDP sections3–17, including child section9, eighteen months after Gazette publication, scheduled13May2027 in the current integration assessment. They are not already operative on21November2026. Existing SPDI/password and contract-capacity duties remain relevant meanwhile. [Official commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf).

## Research boundary and evidence quality

Search excerpts were used where PDFs were robot-gated/403/timed out; those limits are disclosed. An official draft announcement does not prove the absence of subsequent legislation. Articles/thresholds not actually consolidated are left unresolved; this document does not convert unresearched matters into requirements.
