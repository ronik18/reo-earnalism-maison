# Global launch: small, explicit compliance boundary

Worldwide product availability is the objective. Content rights, commerce eligibility and privacy duties must be checked independently; no blanket global clearance is inferred from a public-domain label or one country's approval.

For every edition, retain private evidence by territory, language, format and action. The Selfish Giant has a named-country original-English Reader assessment and an active47-region whitelist; UAE/Mainland China are out of scope and all pending regions are excluded. See SELFISH_GIANT_TERRITORIES.md. No source illustrations, modern adaptation, audio or cover license is inferred. Manifest language claiming worldwide rights is superseded by the narrower retained approved record for this draft. Oscar Wilde died1900 and this text was published1888; original-text analysis is separate from rights in new assets.

Production currently fails closed without a qualified trusted territory adapter. Never authorize from a client country query or browser setting. Before global activation, qualify a trusted edge country signal, unknown-country denial, rights-aware cache expiry, private grants and withdrawal behavior. One centralized policy service and PostgreSQL rights records suffice; no additional compliance microservice is needed.

Before enabling countries, record an explicit supported-country list and review the applicable copyright/public-domain position, privacy/data-transfer requirements, consumer cancellation/refunds, accessibility, tax and payment-provider availability. Prefer a small list of enabled countries expanding with evidence over unqualified worldwide checkout. The selected payment provider must supply country-specific capabilities; recurring/international billing remain disabled meanwhile.

Keep data collection minimal, necessary session cookies, optional analytics/marketing off by default, documented retention and tested export/deletion/account security. Qualify subprocessors, data-processing terms and international-transfer arrangements before production personal-data processing. Preserve the existing no-child-directed-service scope; a fairy tale does not authorize minor profiles or child-data collection. Publish clear privacy, terms, seller/contact, cancellation/refund and accessibility information reviewed for the actual launch scope.

This is an engineering readiness plan, not completed legal sign-off. Resolve evidence for the actual enabled-country/content/commerce scope before activation; obtain qualified advice where an unresolved statutory requirement needs it.
