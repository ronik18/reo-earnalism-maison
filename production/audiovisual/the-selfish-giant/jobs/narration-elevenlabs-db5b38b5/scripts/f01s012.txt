[softly] “We don’t know,” answered the children; “he has gone away.”

[warmly] “You must tell him to be sure and come here to-morrow,” said the Giant. [pause] But the children said that they did not know where he lived, and had never seen him before; and the Giant felt very sad.

[pause] [warmly] Every afternoon, when school was over, the children came and played with the Giant. [sad] But the little boy whom the Giant loved was never seen again. The Giant was very kind to all the children, yet he longed for his first little friend, and often spoke of him. [softly] [sad] “How I would like to see him!” he used to say.
