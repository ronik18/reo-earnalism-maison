[sad] But the Spring never came, nor the Summer. The Autumn gave golden fruit to every garden, but to the Giant’s garden she gave none. [softly] “He is too selfish,” she said. [pause] So it was always Winter there, and the North Wind, and the Hail, and the Frost, and the Snow danced about through the trees.

[long pause] [softly] One morning the Giant was lying awake in bed when he heard some lovely music. It sounded so sweet to his ears that he thought it must be the King’s musicians passing by. [warmly] It was really only a little linnet singing outside his window, but it was so long since he had heard a bird sing in his garden that it seemed to him to be the most beautiful music in the world. [pause] [softly] Then the Hail stopped dancing over his head, and the North Wind ceased roaring, and a delicious perfume came to him through the open casement. [excited] “I believe the Spring has come at last,” said the Giant; and he jumped out of bed and looked out.
