## Source gap narrowed: the grievance rule itself is available

Earlier reviews recorded failed retrieval of a government-hosted full SPDI PDF. The [WIPO Lex official legislative repository](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/15063) now provided the Gazette notification G.S.R.313(E), including Rule5(9): designate/publish the grievance contact and redress processing grievances expeditiously within one month of receipt. This is the 2011 text reproduced by WIPO, **not a new consolidated-law or DPDP commencement determination**. The retained India-first applicability assessment remains separate.

The [MeitY answer to Lok Sabha Question1522, 10 February2021](https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/175/AU1522.pdf?source=pqals) independently confirms the same designation/publication and receipt-based one-month duty. It is historical government confirmation, not a 2026 amendment. Do not cite the different 2011 intermediary rules as this SPDI rule's legal source or infer a different intermediary role without reviewing actual functions.

**Practical conclusion:** the existing notice's “one month from receipt” wording has direct textual support. Do not replace it with a universal 30-day timer, restart the clock after identity checks, or treat merely acknowledging an email as completed redress.
