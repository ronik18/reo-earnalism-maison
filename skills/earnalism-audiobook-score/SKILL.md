# Earnalism narration and score

Use the [shared Maison audiovisual pipeline](https://github.com/ronik18/reo-earnalism-maison/blob/main/docs/EARNALISM_AUDIOVISUAL_PIPELINE.md)
with the audiovisual, voice and video skills. Resolve one exact content edition,
selected narration and destination set; reuse the same production record. Read
[retained learning](references/learning.md), the current title feedback and the
[scoring guide](references/audiobook_background_score.md) for the existing schema
and offline renderer. Historical repository commands require their actual checkout;
this skill does not create a new composer, publication controller or paid call.

For new narration use the fixed final voice bank from the audiobook skill.
Preserve accepted exact audio and title pins; score work never changes words,
speaker, pacing or narration timing. Open Window retains its exact selected
soundtrack/style and candidate-bound KEEP passages. Keep trials separate.

Read the complete source and direction. Map the work/chapter, paragraph, sentence
and emotional turn to the measured narration clock. Separate character intent,
narrative tone and speech act: a question need not be suspense, and serious
content need not be sad or silent. Select a restrained, cleared instrumental and
ambience palette that supports the content's energy and setting. Record source,
licence, permitted uses, modifications and actual stem capacity; do not invent a
missing asset or relabel an unsuitable historical palette.

Freeze suitable dry narration before cue placement. Bind source text, native
narration, measured timings and cue/stem hashes. Choose starts, musical phrase
joins, fades, transitions and speech ducking for the actual material. Keep voice
clear through consonants and emotional peaks; gently join compatible score
phrases and avoid accidental dropouts. Do not apply a universal gain, fade time
or silence rule as a substitute for the scene's requirements.

Use purposeful silence for literary turns and quiet passages. Preserve the
continuous science body accompaniment rule where applicable, with gentle body
edge fades. Transition around chapter/scene boundaries without cutting words or
letting the body score bleed into the exact approved END sound. Narration changes
invalidate old timing/cue lineage; unchanged narration and KEEP sections can reuse
valid evidence. Separate dry masters from scored distribution derivatives.

For a narrow retained-baseline placement repair, use the guide's existing
`render --edits` route with raw baseline-plan SHA, actual feedback rationale and
complete placements. Preserve the baseline and inspect the emitted effective plan.
Do not silently rewrite its history or regenerate healthy narration. Revalidate
only changed joins and their real dependencies plus required final checks.

Measure actual duration, levels, clipping, coverage, source capacity and speech
masking. Assess the full resulting mix for intelligibility, continuity and heard
emotional fit; waveform/ASR/music-presence labels cannot supply that listening.
Reuse relevant existing evidence and avoid repeated reviewer calls chasing a
verdict. An inconclusive result stays inconclusive, and no external attempt or
spending scope is created by this instruction.

For cinematic output, hand the exact scored master and measured cues to the video
compositor. Inspect synchronization in each requested aspect composition. One
final candidate/destination decision covers the complete release package; music
rights must cover its actual audiovisual synchronization/distribution use. Keep
private until the applicable supported release operation and authority exist.

Retain candidate hash, reviewer/method, time scope, observed finding, KEEP/fix
decisions, resulting hash and actual verification/listening status in the same
production record. Promote only evidenced reusable lessons or explicit preferences
into retained learning; keep title facts in their record and use `no-new-learning`
when appropriate. Finish each bounded pass with accepted/held/failed scope.
This improves direction/configuration; it does not train model weights or promise
perfect score fit. The [installed entrypoint snapshot](references/installed-entrypoint-20261121.md)
preserves earlier recipes and provenance without making legacy paths Maison APIs.


For private output delivery, use the common source-bound bundle and
[Colab-to-Maison/social handoff](https://github.com/ronik18/reo-earnalism-maison/blob/main/docs/EARNALISM_PRIVATE_HANDOFF.md).
Prepare verifies retained bytes without provider requests. Explicit private staging
reuses existing draft intake and nonpublic provider adapters; it does not grant
publication, rights or performance acceptance. Reuse measured timing and original
assets; never recreate narration/music/visuals merely to transfer them.
