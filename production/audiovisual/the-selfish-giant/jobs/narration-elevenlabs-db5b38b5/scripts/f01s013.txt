[slowly] Years went over, and the Giant grew very old and feeble. He could not play about any more, so he sat in a huge armchair, and watched the children at their games, and admired his garden. [elderly voice] [warmly] “I have many beautiful flowers,” he said; “but the children are the most beautiful flowers of all.”

[long pause] [softly] One winter morning he looked out of his window as he was dressing. He did not hate the Winter now, for he knew that it was merely the Spring asleep, and that the flowers were resting.
