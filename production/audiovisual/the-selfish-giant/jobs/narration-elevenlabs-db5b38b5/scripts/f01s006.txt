[dramatically] So the Hail came. Every day for three hours he rattled on the roof of the castle till he broke most of the slates, and then he ran round and round the garden as fast as he could go. [pause] He was dressed in grey, and his breath was like ice.

[pause] [tired] “I cannot understand why the Spring is so late in coming,” said the Selfish Giant, as he sat at the window and looked out at his cold white garden; [sighs] “I hope there will be a change in the weather.”
