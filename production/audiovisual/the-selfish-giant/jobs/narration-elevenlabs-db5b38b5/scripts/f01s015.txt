[angry] [deep voice] “Who hath dared to wound thee?” cried the Giant; “tell me, that I may take my big sword and slay him.”

[pause] [softly] “Nay!” answered the child; “but these are the wounds of Love.”

[pause] [trembling voice] “Who art thou?” said the Giant, [softly] and a strange awe fell on him, and he knelt before the little child.

[long pause] [warmly] [softly] And the child smiled on the Giant, and said to him, “You let me play once in your garden, to-day you shall come with me to my garden, which is Paradise.”

[long pause] [softly] [slowly] And when the children ran in that afternoon, they found the Giant lying dead under the tree, all covered with white blossoms.
