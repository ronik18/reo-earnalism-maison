Do not claim today's SPDI duties repealed, or impose future section 9 child flows as already operative.

The [2011 Gazette rules, rules 3, 5, 7 and 8](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/494931) classify passwords as sensitive, require electronic purpose consent before collection, a published named grievance officer/contact, grievance redress within one month, proportionate security and a protected overseas-transfer route. [MeitY's official clarification](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=74990) accepts electronic consent and distinguishes direct services to individuals from the business-to-business processing exemption. A free direct reader service does not inherit that exemption merely because current users are staff.

Password-purpose choice is prospective, explicit and versioned; an automatically populated old notice-version row is not prior affirmative consent. A declined choice stops that password action, not public catalog browsing.
