# Public all-ages qualification: EMEA / Central Asia transfer group

Research date: 21 November 2026. DRAFT ONLY. Countries: RU, KZ, UZ, TR, AM, AZ, BH, SA, OM, QA, JO, IL, GE, IQ, LB, YE.

This is a bounded primary-source issue map for a prospective public, free, all-ages Earnalism offer. It does not certify these 16 countries, the complete 47-country scope, or any particular book. Existing isolated technical acceptance is separate. No code, country whitelist, provider, filings, payments, production records, book draft or legacy platform changed. Physical Android/iOS UAT remains deferred and non-blocking.

## Decision and territorial scope

Public all-ages qualification is **not established**. Free pricing does not itself create an exception from personal-data, child-consent, capacity or notice duties. An ordinary account needs email, authentication/device/session information and generates security records; these must be included with profiles, learning/progress, support, CDN/media and backups in the data map. A child-facing guardian flow must be assessed before collecting those data, not merely before showing a book.

A published country offer/whitelist plus accepted registrations is stronger evidence of intended service than a foreign page being technically reachable. The statutes below use different triggers: citizenship (notably RU localization/UZ), residents (SA), territorial processing/technical means (GE/BH), and domestic database/export (IL). A public offer is not a universal statutory trigger, nor is a foreign hosting address a universal escape. Determine actual controller identity, establishment, local technical means/cookies, customers and each processor location. Do not use IP country as proof of citizenship, residence or legal capacity. These are applicability issues to resolve, not automatic conclusions that every accessible website must register everywhere.

## Country findings and smallest concrete requirements

### RU — collection localization is a substantive unresolved architecture issue

152-FZ article 18(5), as amended by 23-FZ effective 1 July 2025, prohibits the listed collection-stage operations concerning Russian citizens using databases outside Russia, subject to specified article 6 exceptions. It is stronger than merely keeping a later domestic copy. Article 12 separately regulates cross-border transfers; article 22 covers processing notification. A foreign-first account intake must not be declared compliant from encryption, an EU SCC or a checkbox. Identify applicable article 1 scope and the exact collection/export sequence, operator notifications and recipient routes before public qualification. Minor account/contract capacity and guardian authority remain unqualified in this pass.

Primary: [Ministry-hosted 152-FZ text](https://mintrud.gov.ru/docs/laws/130) (indexed official text retrieved; full-page fetch timed out); [Moscow official statutory PDF](https://www.mos.ru/upload/documents/files/8132/Federalniizakonot27iulya2006g152-FZOpersonalnihdannih.pdf). [Official 23-FZ publication](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202502280034) was located but fetch timed out; do not portray it as fully read. Verify current consolidation before implementation.

### KZ — domestic storage and consent/capacity must be separated

Law 94-V article 12(2) requires domestic storage; current official text includes a database and/or digital object located in Kazakhstan. Article 16 governs cross-border transfers, including protection and statutory exceptions; article 8 requires subject/legal-representative consent as applicable. Singapore storage alone is not proved sufficient. Establish local storage applicability/implementation and separate export basis. Civil Code articles 22–23 distinguish 14–18-year-olds and under-14s, with legal-representative and limited transaction rules; do not treat a zero-price ongoing account as automatically a small immediately performed household transaction.

Primary: [current official data law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z1300000094); [official Civil Code](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K940001000_); [government Civil Code extract](https://www.gov.kz/org/ardfm/documents/details/74284?lang=ru). Exact foreign-operator territorial application and any current registration duty require confirmation.

### UZ — March 2026 amendment changes the answer; foreign route still needs proof

ZRU-1125, effective 27 March 2026, replaces article 27¹. Mandatory domestic storage concerns biometric, genetic and specified domestic telecommunications-user data. Other data can be stored/processed abroad through an approved adequate-country route, authority-compliant standard clauses/binding corporate rules, or authority-listed international management/storage standards. Article 20 registration is narrowed to the mandatory-local databases. Do not repeat an obsolete blanket localization conclusion. Conversely, do not assume generic EU SCCs or vendor ISO claims satisfy Uzbekistan's authority-approved requirements. Obtain the current implementing lists/instruments and map Maison fields/vendors; article 21 requires parental/guardian written consent, including electronic documents, for minors; the applicable minor-age/civil-capacity definition still needs confirmation.

Primary: [official amendment, article 1 replacing articles 20 and 27¹](https://lex.uz/docs/8104580), [official effective-date card](https://www.lex.uz/ru/actinfo/card1/8104580), [official law article 21, September 2026 consolidation](https://www.lex.uz/ru/docs/4396428?ONDATE=12.09.2026+02). Russian rendering is explicitly an unofficial translation on the official portal; verify Uzbek operative text for a final decision.

### TR — recurring cloud transfers need the Turkish route

Law 6698 article 9 (2024 amendment) permits adequacy or specified appropriate safeguards with required underlying processing conditions. Its own standard contracts require notification within five business days of signature; exceptional consent routes are for incidental transfers, not a ready-made recurring cloud architecture. Establish Turkish SCC module/parties and notification receipt if relying on that route. Assess article 16 VERBIS registration and foreign-controller representative under the Registry By-Law; do not assume domestic small-business thresholds exempt a foreign operator. No fixed child digital-consent age was verified here: civil capacity and guardian authority require separate analysis.

Primary: [KVKK consolidated law, articles 5, 9, 16](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6649/Personal-Data-Protection-Law), [official 2024 transfer explanation](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/2053/Yurtdisina-Aktarim), [Registry By-Law](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6635/By-Law-On-Data-Controllers-Registry), [registration guidance](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6602/Obligation-to-Register-with-the-Data-Controllers-Registry).

### AM — under-16 representative consent; permission cannot be replaced by consent alone

Personal-data law article 9 provides legal-representative consent for under-16s and incapable/limited-capacity subjects. Article 27 distinguishes adequate destinations and transfers to non-adequate states needing authority permission plus approved contractual safeguards. Do not read article 27(1)'s consent/purpose language as eliminating article 27(3)–(4). Verify destination status/permissions and foreign-operator scope. Civil contract-capacity analysis remains separate.

Primary: [official consolidated Armenian law](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/218690), [official-hosted English translation, articles 9 and 27](https://www.arlis.am/Annexes/4/Law_Personal_data_protection_EN.pdf).

### AZ — transfer, written consent and system registration need actual assessment

Personal-data law article 14 prohibits specified unsafe exports but provides consent/vital-interest exceptions; security responsibility remains. Articles 8–9 govern consent and collection/processing, and article 15 system registration/exceptions must be assessed for the actual database. Do not assume all systems are exempt or all foreign websites are automatically registered. Establish required consent form/representative authority and applicable registry exception or filing; exact child/contract-age rules and foreign-operator nexus remain unqualified.

Primary: [official law, articles 8–9, 14–15](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/19/f_19675.html).

### BH — means-in-country trigger; representative is conditional

Law 30/2018 article 2 includes foreign persons using means in Bahrain other than mere transit, and requires a representative for the specified foreign legal persons. Articles 12–13 regulate exports; articles 14–15 establish notification/authorization rules and exceptions. First determine whether Maison's technical means and actual operator satisfy that trigger; mere worldwide availability is not the text of the rule. Establish an applicable transfer route, notification/exception and representative only where required. Child capacity/consent and current adequacy/order exceptions remain unqualified.

Primary: [official Arabic consolidated law](https://lloc.gov.bh/Legislation/HTM/K3018), [government English text](https://www.bahrain.bh/wps/wcm/connect/ab8b334e-8c6f-4ff9-90b6-94135da559ca/Law%2BNo.%2B%2830%29%2Bof%2B2018%2BDPL.pdf?CVID=oFapPNI&MOD=AJPERES).

### SA — residents outside-establishment reach; guardian and Saudi transfer safeguards

PDPL article 2 extends to foreign processing of personal data relating to individuals residing in the Kingdom. Article 5 and implementing regulation article 3 address consent from legal guardians and verification of guardianship where capacity is lacking. Article 29 and the updated transfer regulation govern foreign processing, onward safeguards and relevant risk assessment. Map recipients, use Saudi-authorized safeguards and document legal basis; ordinary EU SCCs are not by themselves a completed Saudi route. Current controller-registration/DPO criteria and individual child capacity thresholds require final checking; no blanket local-representative claim is made.

Primary: [government PDPL](https://misa.gov.sa/app/uploads/2025/08/PersonalDataProtectionLaw.pdf), [authority regulations portal](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPL2/), [official announcement identifying September 2024 updated transfer regulation](https://spa.gov.sa/en/w2163905). Final instrument: https://sdaia.gov.sa/Documents/RegulationonPersonalDataEN.pdf (linked by official announcement; version completion must be checked before execution).

### OM — guardian consent before processing, including sharing

PDPL article 6 and regulation 34/2024 articles 11–15 require guardian involvement for children, including explicit consent before processing and sharing, minimal guardian details to verify authority, and guardian access. Regulation cross-border safeguards and the authority's current FAQ distinguish ordinary-data transfers from sensitive-data approvals; do not label all foreign transfers approval-required or all child data automatically sensitive-permit data. Implement demonstrable guardian consent/control and recipient safeguards. Confirm foreign-controller scope and operative age/capacity definition.

Primary: [official regulation overview](https://mtcit.gov.om/library-3/legislations-policies-8/regulations-72/executive-regulations-of-the-personal-data-protection-law-1035), [official English regulation, articles 11–15](https://www.ita.gov.om/itaportal/Data/English/DocLibrary/2024115131527120/Issuing%20the%20Executive%20Regulation%20of%20the%20Personal%20Data%20Protection%20Law.pdf), [current authority transfer/permit FAQ](https://www.mtcit.gov.om/sectors/governance/personal).

### QA — all-ages offer requires child-facing assessment, not an adult checkbox

Law 13/2016 article 17 expressly addresses websites directed at children: child-data notice, explicit guardian consent, verified guardian access/deletion and no excess-data condition for participation. Article 15 treats child data as special-nature data and requires applicable competent-department permission. Resolve that permission and the child-facing characterization before accepting child accounts. Cross-border processing article 13 has a distinct structure, not a general compulsory local-hosting rule. Actual foreign-operator scope and age/capacity still need verification.

Primary: [official article 17](https://www.almeezan.qa/LawArticles.aspx?LawArticleID=72903&LawID=7121&language=ar), [authority law copy](https://assurance.ncsa.gov.qa/sites/default/files/library/2020-11/Law%20No.%20%2813%29%20of%202016%20%20on%20Protecting%20Personal%20Data%20Privacy%20-%20Arabic.pdf), [authority child-notice guidance](https://assurance.ncsa.gov.qa/sites/default/files/library/2022-12/Privacy%20Notice%20-%20Guideline%20for%20Regulated%20Entities.pdf).

### JO — guardian consent and destination assessment

Law 24/2023 article 5 addresses valid prior consent and guardian involvement where legal capacity is absent (full official PDF fetch timed out during this pass; confirm the operative clause before final approval); articles 14–15 regulate sharing and foreign transfers/protection exceptions. The ministry describes scope as citizens/residents within Jordan and confirms the general transition ended in March 2025. Map lawful basis/recipient disclosures, confirm destination protection or exact exception, and assess DPO/permit implementing rules. No generic Singapore adequacy or free-service exemption is established here.

Primary: [ministry FAQ](https://modee.gov.jo/EN/Pages/FAQs), [official law, articles 5, 14–15](https://www.modee.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/ar/eb_list_page/personal_data_protection_law_no.24_of_2023.pdf), [current implementing-law index](https://www.modee.gov.jo/AR/List/القوانين_والأنظمة_و_التعليمات_الصادرة_بمقتضاه). Guardian age and foreign-offer nexus need final confirmation.

### IL — do not use obsolete general database registration threshold

Amendment 13 narrowed registration; current authority guidance explicitly supersedes the former general rule. Assess section 8A registration/notification and section 17B1 DPO triggers using the real database purpose/scale. Foreign-transfer regulations 1–3 specifically address export from databases in Israel; do not automatically equate an Israeli visitor submitting directly abroad with export from a domestic database. If applicable, select a lawful route and obtain the recipient undertaking; the authority issued updated regulation 2(4) interpretation in April 2026. Minor consent/capacity and broader foreign-operator scope remain unresolved.

Primary: [current registration guidance](https://www.gov.il/he/service/registration_in_the_database), [government-hosted transfer regulations](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/legislation/en/PrivacyProtectionTransferofDataabroadRegulationsun.pdf), [2026 authority interpretation](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/legalinfo/infoabroad/en/infoabroad.pdf) (Hebrew document despite /en path).

### GE — current consolidated source found; local technical-means trigger

Current law article 2 includes foreign controllers using technical means in Georgia except transit. Article 7 sets consent at 16 with parental/legal-representative consent below 16 and child-interest constraints. Article 34 requires applicable foreign technical-means operators to register a local representative before processing, with EU/recognized adequate-state establishment exceptions; article 37 governs transfer safeguards/permission. Current consolidated text names the State Audit Office: do not reuse the former regulator's identity blindly. Establish technical-means scope and recipient route; academic/literary-purpose exceptions cannot simply exempt ordinary library account/security administration. Contract capacity is separate.

Primary: [official final consolidated English text, articles 2, 7, 34, 37](https://matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/5827307). Earlier ?publication=7 is expressly non-final and was not used as the final-version claim.

### LB — declaration exemptions need exact application, not automatic filing

Law 81/2018 articles 94–96 provide declaration exemptions, declaration procedure and details including a foreign controller's representative. Article 98 underlies the ministry's public processing list. Assess the exact article 94 exemption first; do not automatically demand declaration for every consent-based processing or treat foreign incorporation as automatic immunity. Child capacity, relevant transfer conditions and foreign-offer nexus were not fully resolved in this bounded pass.

Primary: [Central Bank-hosted law, articles 94–98](https://www.bdl.gov.lb/CB%20Com/Laws%20And%20Regulations/Laws/Law_81_AR%C2%A72572_1.pdf), [Ministry's active article 98 processing-list service](https://www.economy.gov.lb/ar/services/البيانات-ذات-الطابع-الشخصي).

### IQ and YE — unresolved, not permission by absence

Iraq's constitution article 17 protects personal privacy; article 40 protects communications. Yemen's published constitution article 53 protects communications confidentiality. These are primary baselines, not proof that no data/consumer/child rule exists, no localization applies, or any public foreign offer is legal. This pass did not verify a current comprehensive child-consent/contract-capacity/foreign-transfer/registration path or territorial consumer regime in either country. Check current operative legislation, applicable territorial authority and service/content restrictions before claiming public all-ages qualification. Do not invent a numerical child threshold or a generic representative requirement.

Primary: [Iraq Parliament constitution](https://iq.parliament.iq/الدستور-العراقي/), [Yemen National Information Center constitution](https://www.yemen-nic.info/contents/laws_ye/dostor.php?ID=5846&login=yes&print=Y).
