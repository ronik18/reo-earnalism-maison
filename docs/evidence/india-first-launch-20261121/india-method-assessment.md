## Current law versus future rules

The [official commencement notification GSR843(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) puts substantive DPDP sections3–17, including section9, and section44(2) in the eighteen-month tranche. They are not operative on21November2026. [MeitY's current parliamentary answer](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2202901&lang=2&reg=48) expressly says SPDI governs during this implementation period. Future Rule10 identifiable-adult/authorized-identity/token provisions are not a present mandate to integrate Aadhaar, DigiLocker, KWS, cards or face scanning.

Current [SPDI Gazette313(E), government copy](https://dispur.nic.in/itact/it-procedures-sensitive-personal-data-rules-2011.pdf) classifies passwords as sensitive and requires purpose-specific prior consent, necessity, privacy disclosure, correction/withdrawal, limited retention, grievance handling, safeguards and a protected transfer route. Today's full PDF fetch timed out; the source's indexed official text and the retained operative assessment were available. The [official24August2011 clarification](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=74990) accepts electronic communication for consent. No reviewed current SPDI provision prescribes external identity verification for every reader or every parent. This does **not** mean a child's checkbox conclusively proves valid authority for every purpose.

[Contract Act10–11, India Code](https://indiacode.gov.in/server/api/core/bitstreams/e7571d39-11a7-4a2c-86e3-c515ae43e617/content) requires competent contracting parties; the minor cannot be treated as independently competent merely because the service is free or the reader is17. India's general majority rule is18 for an Indian-domiciled person; domicile and location are not identical. The [IT Act10A government-hosted text](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15983/1/the_information_technology_act%2C_2008.pdf) recognizes electronic contract formation but does not cure lack of capacity; direct PDF fetch timed out, official indexed clause was readable. No blanket free-library exemption is claimed.

## Recommended smallest direct method now

**Recommendation:** support adult self-enrollment and a parent/legal-guardian-sponsored account through the same reader experience, using existing verified mailbox/session controls and purpose-bound electronic permission. Describe it as **parent-declared electronic authorization**, not independently verified adulthood/custody or DPDP VPC certification. This is an implementable proportionate present-law recommendation; the exact current safeguards and authority facts below must match the delivered flow.

1. Before new account credentials or optional Google redirect, ask the minimum capacity/country choice: India service scope, enrolling for self with18+ capacity, or parent/legal guardian opening a child's account. An under18 self-reader is directed to the sponsor route rather than falsely declaring independent capacity. Do not collect full DOB, national ID, child's name/email or passwords just to decide the route. Existing operator adult-tester facts need not be recollected globally.
2. Sponsor first controls a verified adult-claimed account/mailbox and expressly declares18+ capacity and parent/legal-guardian authority for the particular child account. Show the free-service terms to that adult, who accepts responsibility in their own capacity; do not claim this makes the child personally bound to adult contractual liabilities. Record notice/purpose, exact declaration/version, sponsor ID and timestamp. Mailbox control plus an age declaration is evidence of the communication and claim, **not** an independent identity/custody check.
3. Obtain affirmative, unbundled permission for the described child account/reading/security purposes before entering the child's data; retain an opaque prospective-subject slot. Include password processing when the parent creates it, actual recipients/locations and applicable transfer permission. Do not rely exclusively on a supposedly enforceable minor/free-service contract to satisfy Rule7 necessity: use explicit informed transfer permission for this sponsored route while still requiring same-level protection. No ad/marketing approval is implied.
4. Bind the approval to the **exact intended account** at final claim, with sponsor confirmation of the child email/identifier and permitted purposes before creation. Retain a keyed digest/reference rather than duplicating child data in logs. Confirm mailbox ownership after creation if the account uses email; do not link an existing account by guessed email. Changing the intended child/account invalidates or requires renewed sponsor confirmation. A successful mailbox check alone proves no family relationship.
5. Permit the same free reader UI after enrollment. Keep optional choices off, private notes private, and separate a sponsor's account-processing authorization from a request to see/export/delete the child's information. Provide direct correction/withdrawal, suspension/revocation, limited evidence retention and grievance handling. On authority dispute, contradictory signals or sensitive requests, hold the affected operation and obtain proportionate manual evidence privately; do not collect government IDs from everyone by default.

The sources support electronic consent and a non-vendor implementation; they do not provide a statutory safe-harbor formula saying any self-declared parent plus email is always sufficient. Do not market such an absolute claim. A truthful low-risk free-reading flow with no charges, public social contact or default profiling is narrower than a verifier vendor's generalized product. Any later sensitive feature or fraud evidence needs its own risk response. Current purpose consent for adult passwords must remain independent and prospective.
