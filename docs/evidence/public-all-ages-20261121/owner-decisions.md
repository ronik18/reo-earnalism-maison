21 November 2026. **Owner approved the minimum child flow and regional design preparation in this chat.** Purchases, appointments, filings, production migrations and country removals remain excluded. This package preserves **all47 countries, free access, all-ages reading intent and one English navigation journey**. It does not propose silently removing a country, banning children or reintroducing language-specific UI journeys. ## Decision 1: minimum child-compatible account design

Approved preparation/implementation direction: authorize designing and implementing the minimum child/guardian authorization and rights journey where the established national rule and selected purpose/ground require it. Preserve a single reader experience. Apply high privacy defaults to everyone; collect only the minimum age/capacity/authority evidence required by the actual route, separate optional choices and keep unnecessary documents out of the application. Do not treat a guardian email checkbox as verified permission or apply one consent age globally.

Concrete acceptance: required authority precedes covered collection; reject forged/replayed/expired authority; bind grants to child/purpose/notice version; verify guardian rights without disclosing unrelated private notes; support revocation, export/delete and backup reconciliation. Local thresholds, method and retention must be resolved before coding their final rules. This is **not a request to approve a legally unverified method**. Broader dashboards, identity vendors and new spending are excluded.

An alternate low-data guest-reading design could reduce account collection but would alter existing protected member access and still expose request metadata. It cannot be called a zero-data shortcut or exempt from regional duties. Do not implement that alternate without a separate concrete access/rights design approval.

## What approval cannot replace

Owner approval cannot resolve an unknown law, supply parental authority, appoint a representative without an actual mandate, or waive a mandatory storage rule. Source retrieval/translation limits, current US-state/provincial and national consumer/capacity questions listed in the assessments remain work. Obtain targeted qualified local interpretation where authoritative research cannot establish a necessary conclusion; present the exact engagement/cost before spending.

Until those conclusions, controls and receipts exist, retain the currently qualified isolated scope and truthful public notices. Push this assessment for review, but do not describe a documentation push or unchanged runtime deployment as public/all-ages legal qualification. Physical Android/iOS UAT remains deferred/non-blocking. Book draft, legacy application/data/DNS and paid flags remain untouched.
