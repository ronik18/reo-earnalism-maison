## Brazil: representative purchase is not automatic

[Consolidated ECA Digital](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2025/lei/l15211.htm), Articles39–40, must be read together. Article39 includes Article40 among duties editorial/licensed-content providers may be dispensed from under its specified conditions. This creates a concrete alternative to automatically buying a Brazil representative; it is not a blanket literary exemption. Relevant conditions include age-rating transparency, suitable parental technical mediation, appropriate controls and reporting. Current source does not establish the full accommodation. A generic guardian grant is not proof all those conditions are fulfilled. Complete a function-by-function Article39 checklist before either claiming the exemption or presenting a representative appointment for approval. LGPD best interests, applicable child grounds, notices and transfers remain separate.

## Child method and authority

A prospectively random subject slot can avoid child name/email/DOB during an authorization request, but does not itself prove parental relationship or make transport metadata anonymous. A reviewer digest proves a binding to reviewed evidence only; the actual method, guardian authority and national legal basis must be qualified separately. Internal-operations processors are distinguished from recipients with secondary purposes. Plain verified email, a checkbox, or an unqualified hosting contract is not a universal parent-verification method. No global age threshold or universal manual method is approved by this document.
