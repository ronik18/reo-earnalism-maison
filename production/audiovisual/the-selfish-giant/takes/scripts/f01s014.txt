[surprised] Suddenly he rubbed his eyes in wonder, and looked and looked. It certainly was a marvellous sight. [softly] In the farthest corner of the garden was a tree quite covered with lovely white blossoms. Its branches were all golden, and silver fruit hung down from them, and underneath it stood the little boy he had loved.

[pause] [excited] Downstairs ran the Giant in great joy, and out into the garden. He hastened across the grass, and came near to the child. [pause] And when he came quite close his face grew red with anger, and he said, [angry] [deep voice] “Who hath dared to wound thee?” [pause] [softly] For on the palms of the child’s hands were the prints of two nails, and the prints of two nails were on the little feet.
