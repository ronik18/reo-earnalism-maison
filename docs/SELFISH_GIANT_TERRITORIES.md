## Publication rule

Publish any book only when its exact edition, text, covers/assets and every offered format/action are legally compliant in every active whitelisted region. Evidence must include applicable distribution/public-domain permissions, not only the economic copyright term. If a title fails in one active region, keep that title draft/held and resolve its rights; do not silently shrink the whitelist for the title. New region scope requires explicit owner approval and supported evidence.

The Selfish Giant's existing47 Reader read/preview grants remain **draft content decisions**. They are not relabelled as complete publication compliance. Its text, cover bytes, edition/revision IDs and live pointer are unchanged. The existing browser/cover evidence is reused by hash. The new publication_regions check covers the entire active whitelist, and publication_clearance requires private exact-text/asset evidence for that same scope. No real legal pass has been fabricated. Synthetic local fixtures never establish production compliance.
