## Findings that reduce unnecessary work

An Epic developer organization is not a legal company, a jurisdictional compliance requirement or an Earnalism requirement. It is only KWS/Epic's account-access prerequisite. No national source reviewed requires this particular vendor. The owner has skipped that route; no Epic/KWS setup is proposed here.

**Kazakhstan — no universal card/biometric vendor requirement established.** A [26March2026 government explanation](https://www.gov.kz/org/aktobe-alga/press/article/details/229933?lang=ru) describes subject/legal-representative consent through writing, electronic document or other protected actions, with OTP/SMS/click examples. It also describes operator proof of consent, purpose/data/recipient/export details, and domestic storage including for nonresident operators. This supports evaluating a recorded electronic authorization without introducing card/face collection. It does **not** prove that a checkbox identifies the correct legal representative or that every foreign service is in scope. The [ministry's2023 guidance](https://www.gov.kz/org/maidd/press/article/details/118745) separately discusses foreign systems plus transfer consent and domestic storage; it therefore does not support treating Kazakhstan as identical to Russia's collection-stage rule. These are government explanations, not a fresh consolidated2026 statutory text or a provider-placement receipt. Canonical Adilet was JavaScript-only and old-Adilet returned502 in this follow-up. Current Article12 digital-object wording and exact service nexus remain unconfirmed from a successfully fetched consolidation.

**Uzbekistan — ordinary data does not automatically require a new domestic stack.** The [official Uzbek consolidated law](https://www.lex.uz/docs/4396419), Article27¹, confirms the March2026 narrower mandatory-local categories: biometric, genetic and specified domestic telecom-user data. Other records can follow one of its authorized foreign-processing routes; Article20 narrows registration accordingly. Article21 permits parental/guardian written consent including electronic documents for minors. The operative Uzbek clauses were retrieved directly, resolving the earlier Russian-translation-only limitation. [Amendment record](https://lex.uz/docs/8104580) gives27March2026 effectiveness. **Inference:** avoid adding biometrics or automatically purchasing Uzbek hosting; first map existing fields/recipients and establish the actual approved foreign instrument. This pass did not find the authority's complete current approved-country/standard-clause/standards lists, or settle civil-capacity transitions. No generic ISO certificate or EU SCC is marked sufficient.

**Türkiye — foreign-small-operator exemption cannot be assumed.** [Authority decision2019/225](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/5545/2019-225) distinguishes foreign controllers from independently acting Turkish branches; its conclusion requires registration for foreign controllers processing directly or through branches in Türkiye, while branch-size assessment is separate. Thus Reo's small size alone does not close registration. Newer [official2025 exemption material](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8388/KAMUOYU-DUYURUSU) was located in indexed official results, but full-page retrieval timed out; no current exemption is claimed. The [Registry By-Law](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6635/By-Law-On-Data-Controllers-Registry) and [transfer-instrument notice](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8170/Yurt-Disina-Kisisel-Veri-Aktariminda-Kullanilacak-Standart-Sozlesmelerde-Dikkat-Edilmesi-Gereken-Hususlara-Iliskin-Kamuoyu-Duyurusu) also failed refresh. Prior Article9 instrument/notification analysis remains open; nothing justifies buying another database merely to resolve Turkish contracting/registration questions. Reo's proprietorship status also needs its actual natural-person-controller classification rather than assuming it is a foreign corporation.

**Russia — do not replace unavailable source verification with a procurement requirement.** The [official23-FZ publication](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202502280034) and [ministry152-FZ page](https://mintrud.gov.ru/docs/laws/130) again timed out. The earlier collection-stage/localization issue therefore remains unresolved, not waived or freshly fully verified. Determine current operative scope, citizenship and exceptions, collection versus later transfer, and notifications before choosing any infrastructure. No universal vendor or foreign-first replication shortcut is established. Public documentation cannot manufacture an actual compliant data path or provider contract.
