## Risk and mitigation decisions

| Concrete risk | Existing useful control | Required assessment/mitigation and acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Child account collected without valid ground or authority | Verified email and explicit password purpose; optional choices off | Country/purpose/capacity classification and proportionate verified guardian route where required; test covered collection occurs only after valid authority and no optional bundling |
| Sensitive reading/notes disclosed on a shared device or to false guardian | Bound private APIs, session revocation, ownership tests and encrypted storage | Child-appropriate notice and recovery/rights threat model; test guardian proof, least disclosure, withdrawal and no resurrection after restore |
| Excessive profiling/evaluation or unclear language | Private reading/learning, no public social feed established | Screen actual quizzes/progress and free text, not invented advertising; minimize and explain in audience-appropriate language; assess necessary PIAs/DPIAs |
| Foreign intake/storage or onward access violates applicable local rule | Existing TLS, private storage, bounded retention | Resolve territorial triggers and actual provider/data path before accepting affected collection; no country-routing-by-IP-as-citizenship assumption |
| Support/rights cannot be exercised effectively | Existing export/delete/withdraw and business contact | Child/guardian-specific safe rights route, applicable response deadline, coverage and understandable local-language instructions |
| Marketing overstates free price, universal legal clearance or availability | Free activation boundary and truthful qualification notice | Review reference-price substantiation and actual terms/consumer rights; no fictitious discounts or universal compliance claims |

Residual risks above are **unresolved**, not accepted. Country law distinguishes access age, contract capacity, data-consent authority and age-appropriate design. A global age checkbox cannot settle all four. Prefer high privacy for all users and minimal age data, but this does not replace verifiable authority where the selected lawful basis requires it. No identity-document collection, parental dashboard or foreign hosting purchase has been selected.
