[happy] And when the people were going to market at twelve o’clock they found the Giant playing with the children in the most beautiful garden they had ever seen.

[warmly] All day long they played, and in the evening they came to the Giant to bid him good-bye.

[pause] [curious] “But where is your little companion?” he said: “the boy I put into the tree.” [softly] The Giant loved him the best because he had kissed him.
