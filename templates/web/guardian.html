For our free India-first service, a parent or legal guardian who is 18 or older signs in to their own verified account and gives electronic permission before entering the younger reader’s details. We do not ask for a date of birth, ID document, face scan or payment card. A verified mailbox confirms mailbox control; it is not an independent age or custody check.

The account uses an email and protected password for sign-in and security, plus saved books, reading progress, private notes and preferences for requested reading features. Our existing providers process information in Singapore and internationally, as explained in the privacy notice. Parent permission includes these necessary purposes and described overseas processing; optional marketing and analytics remain separate.

Your reading stays personal. Being a parent does not automatically disclose a reader’s private notes. A request to act for another account needs the specific current authority for that request.
