## Narrow source question resolved

Current [16 CFR 312.10](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.10), retrieved today, requires purpose-limited child-data retention, deletion when no longer necessary, and a written policy stating collection purposes, retention need and deletion timeframe. That policy must be included in the service's required notice. It does not supply a universal numerical period. Thus an existing housekeeping timer or an authority expiry is not by itself the complete child-data retention determination. Conversely, it does not establish that every existing timer must automatically be shortened.

Current [16 CFR 312.8](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.8) requires a proportionate written security program, assigned coordination, risk review, safeguards/testing and periodic evaluation, plus reasonable recipient assessment/written security assurances. It does not prescribe buying a new security SaaS or certification. Both eCFR pages display Title 16 current through 19 November 2026 and describe themselves as authoritative but unofficial.
