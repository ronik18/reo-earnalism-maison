# Regional collection, storage and transfer design — preparation draft

21November2026. Owner-approved **design preparation only**, following [owner decisions](owner-decisions.md). No provider selected, bought or contacted; no contract signed, filing made, region migrated, schema changed or access policy altered. This design preserves47countries, all-ages intent, free access, one English navigation journey, protected draft and legacy platform. Physical UAT remains deferred/nonblocking. Root owns integration and any later approved external action.

## Decision to prepare

For records actually subject to domestic collection/storage requirements, prepare a small regional data plane containing the necessary intake, authentication, authoritative record store, local operational records and recovery capability. Keep one codebase/user experience and the current Singapore service for flows legally permitted there. Do not implement a later domestic replica behind an unchanged Singapore-first signup and call it localization. This is a design direction, not approval of a specific multi-region architecture or a conclusion that every Russian/Kazakh visitor triggers every local rule.

A country label or IP route alone is insufficient: Russian localization concerns Russian citizens and relevant statutory scope; Kazakhstan's precise foreign-operator nexus and current wording require a supported determination. Citizenship, residence, physical location and child capacity are different facts. Determine minimum routing/eligibility evidence before covered intake, without routinely requesting identity documents. If classification is unresolved, do not silently send a potentially covered form to the default Singapore backend or silently remove the country; present the unresolved route for an owner decision.

## Russia: collection-first and any later export are separate questions

The research identifies152-FZ Article18(5), amended by23-FZ effective1July2025, restricting listed collection-stage operations for Russian citizens using overseas databases, with specified exceptions; Articles12/22 separately concern transfers/operator notification. [Official23-FZ publication](https://publication.pravo.gov.ru/document/0001202502280034), [ministry-hosted152-FZ](https://mintrud.gov.ru/docs/laws/130). Official full-text fetches timed out again in this pass; obtain the current consolidated operative text and targeted interpretation before implementation acceptance. Do not claim an exception based merely on free price, consent, encryption or Indian incorporation.

**Candidate sequence to qualify:** regional request termination → permitted local processing/validation → local authoritative durable record → separately authorized onward operation, if current law and notification permit that exact operation. This is deliberately a candidate: a first local write is not automatically permission for a mirrored foreign database to participate in continuing collection/update/extraction. Identify precisely which updates/replicas/remote queries count as prohibited collection-stage operations versus permitted later transfer. Resolve citizenship scope, legal exceptions, operator notifications and foreign recipients before accepting the route.

**Rejected shortcut:** Singapore Render receives signup/password/progress, writes Neon and later replicates to Russia. A local copy cannot undo foreign-first collection. A regional reverse proxy that merely forwards plaintext into Singapore before local persistence has the same unresolved defect. A timestamp claiming a local write is insufficient without data-path evidence.

## Kazakhstan: mandatory domestic storage is not identical to the Russian rule

Law94-V Article12(2) requires domestic storage; the current research identifies database and/or digital-object wording. Article16 separately governs cross-border transfers and Article8 consent/representative authority. [Official law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z1300000094), [official alternate portal](https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/Z1300000094). Current portal required JavaScript/alternate fetch failed; exact consolidated applicability remains an explicit final interpretation item.

A domestic authoritative store with local recovery is the conservative engineering candidate, while export/remote access requires its own supported Article16 route. Do **not** assert Kazakhstan has the identical Russian collection-first prohibition without exact authority; conversely, do not assume that any eventual replica or backup satisfies required domestic accumulation/storage. Specify completeness, durability, ongoing update ordering, deletion/hold state and recoverability of the domestic record. Determine foreign-operator nexus, whether a domestic operator/representative is required, and whether the proposed store qualifies under current statutory terms. No local provider or incorporation requirement is assumed selected/approved.

## Uzbekistan: use the amended ordinary-data route before adding hosting

[ZRU1125,26March2026, effective27March](https://www.lex.uz/docs/8104580) replaced Article27¹ and narrowed mandatory local categories: biometric/genetic and specified domestic telecom-user data. Ordinary data may qualify for overseas storage/processing through current authority-recognized country, contractual/BCR or listed-standard routes; registration scope was correspondingly narrowed. Russian text on the official portal is labelled unofficial: verify operative Uzbek text and current implementing lists.

Classify each Earnalism field and provider operation; a normal email/password hash/progress row is not automatically a mandatory-local biometric/telecom record, but a newly proposed facial identity/guardian-verification service could introduce such categories. Avoid adding that collection by default. Confirm any allowed route against the actual foreign contracting entity, all subprocessors and transfers—not a vendor-wide ISO badge or generic EU SCC. If qualifying approved instruments/lists cannot be established, keep the route unresolved and present the exact alternative; do not fabricate an adequacy decision or automatically procure Uzbek hosting.

## Türkiye: recurring cloud processing needs the Turkish instrument

Article9 requires an underlying Articles5/6 ground plus adequacy or an appropriate safeguard; occasional exceptions are not a reliable route for continuous account/cloud operation. [Official framework](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/2053/Yurtdisina-Aktarim), [official contract cautions](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8170/Yurt-Disina-Kisisel-Veri-Aktariminda-Kullanilacak-Standart-Sozlesmelerde-Dikkat-Edilmesi-Gereken-Hususlara-Iliskin-Kamuoyu-Duyurusu). Turkish standard contracts, when used, require notification within5business days of signature. Exact module, exporter/importer role, untouched mandatory language, completed annexes, signatory authority, effective execution/notification and onward processing must be established. Published EU SCCs or DPA incorporation do not establish those facts. Review foreign-controller VERBIS/representative separately; domestic small-business thresholds are not assumed to exempt Reo.

The preparation package should identify whether direct foreign collection, onward cloud processing and remote access constitute each relevant transfer and which party can actually sign the required route. A Singapore project could remain technically usable **if** exact permitted route, notices, child authority and other requirements are established. No current provider has agreed to a Turkish instrument in this task. Obtain exact counterparty acceptance before presenting an execution/filing for owner approval; this document signs nothing.

## Smallest implementation shape to evaluate

1. **Single routing decision before covered intake.** Country/legal-scope routing uses the minimum justified evidence; no account/body/password should first be uploaded to the default region for classification. Cookies/IP/legal declarations and existing account region need explicit treatment. Unknown/mismatched routes produce an intelligible unresolved state with no credential leakage or silent fallback.
2. **Regional ownership boundary.** Assign a stable opaque account-region binding. Store personal account/guardian evidence, progress/notes, auth sessions/devices and mutable rights outcomes in the qualified regional plane. A central registry is not automatically non-personal: account IDs and region mappings can identify/link people. Evaluate whether no registry, or a strictly minimized qualified routing token, suffices.
3. **No speculative distributed rewrite.** Reuse existing tested application code/protocols where qualified. Cross-region dual writes, global personal-data joins and a global shared session store are not prerequisites. Prepare the least deployment/database separation necessary after actual legal/provider selection, with local consistency and supported rights/restore operations. This is a proposed baseline deviation requiring the owner's concrete implementation approval.
4. **Export only explicitly allowed subsets.** Public book assets and genuinely anonymous aggregate counts can remain separate. Pseudonymous user IDs, IPs, private note quotes and tokenized links are still potentially personal. Define allowed egress by field, purpose, recipient and route; never emit raw requests to a global observability sink.
5. **Fail safely at the affected operation.** If a regional store/mail/auth route fails, do not queue personal work into Singapore as an unreviewed backup. Preserve local work only under the approved client-storage design, with honest sync status. A service outage does not authorize changing territorial scope or publishing a draft.

## Logs, CDN, mail and backups: acceptance is end to end

- **Logs:** enumerate TLS/edge/WAF, access/error, application/audit, database query/admin, worker, mail and support evidence. Collect only necessary metadata; ensure redaction happens before foreign storage rather than deleting after upload. Preserve existing India's scoped180day duty and any separately established regional requirement without conflating them. Exact local log location, operator access, source outage/retry and retrieval deadlines require proof.
- **CDN and assets:** public immutable content can be reused when rights allow, but logged IP/device/token requests remain a separate flow. A proxied regional hostname is not evidence the TLS edge or its logs are domestic. Obtain actual routing/termination/log guarantees or prepare an alternative without silently widening paid infrastructure.
- **Mail and identity:** qualify signup/reset/guardian invitation/support workflows, including rejected/expired requests and Sent/recipient copies. A local application that immediately writes email/action-token data to unqualified Workspace or Google identity is incomplete. Do not disable email/OAuth or choose a substitute provider without exact approved behavior and recovery evidence.
- **Backups:** isolate regional schemas/stores from the global full-DB backup job; enforce permitted backup destinations, key custody, retention/holds and access. A regional primary plus nightly unapproved R2 export is not localization acceptance. Test regional restore plus current deletion/guardian-revocation/hold replay before reopening; do not permit the global restore to resurrect exported or erased records.

## Low-data guest alternative and its limits

A guest route could avoid email/password/account/guardian records and server-side progress/notes, and serve only already cleared public content with optional strictly local reading position. It still produces IP/TLS/CDN/security metadata, may set cookies or local identifiers, and can trigger child/service/content rules. Downloaded materials can contain personal reading state; local storage is not automatically outside privacy law.

This is therefore a **separate access-design option to evaluate**, not a zero-data exemption or substitute for47country compliance. Existing protected member/draft/media boundaries cannot simply be opened to guests. Qualify exact content rights, offline policy, metadata/log recipients and age/capacity disclosures. No guest redesign or country/child exclusion is implemented by this preparation.
